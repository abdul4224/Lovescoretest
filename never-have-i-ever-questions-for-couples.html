100 Never Have I Ever Questions for Couples

Never have I ever is an easy, low-pressure game: one person says something they have never done, and anyone who has done it admits it and tells the story. For couples it is a great way to share funny memories, learn new things about each other, and laugh together.

Here are 100 never have I ever questions for couples, grouped by mood. Use them on date night, on a road trip, with friends, or over a video call when you are apart.

How to play never have I ever as a couple

Take turns reading a statement that starts with "Never have I ever". If the statement is true for you, you say so and share the story behind it; if not, you simply pass. Keep score with a point for every confession, or just play for the stories. Skip anything you do not want to talk about, and keep the mood kind and playful.

Tips for a better game

Ask a follow-up question after each confession, because the stories are the best part. Mix funny statements with a few romantic ones so the mood keeps changing. If you want something with more choices afterwards, try our would you rather or truth or dare question lists.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play never have I ever as a couple?

Take turns saying a statement that starts with "Never have I ever". Anyone who has done it admits it and tells the story, and anyone who has not simply passes. Skip anything that feels uncomfortable and keep it playful.

What are good never have I ever questions for couples?

Good questions are funny, relatable, and kind: small embarrassing moments, travel stories, dating memories, and romantic gestures. A mix of light and sweet statements keeps the game fun and helps you learn something new about each other.

Can couples play never have I ever over text or a video call?

Yes. Send one statement at a time by message or read them out on a video call, and share the stories as you go. It works well for long-distance couples.
