How Zodiac Compatibility Works

This tool blends the classic zodiac element groups — fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), and water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) — with a touch of randomness to produce a fun compatibility percentage between 0% and 100%. Everything runs directly in your browser; your sign choices are never sent to a server or saved anywhere.

Signs from the same or naturally complementary elements (like fire and air, or earth and water) tend to trend toward higher scores, while opposite elements add more unpredictability — similar to how popular astrology columns describe these pairings, just condensed into a single number you can share.
