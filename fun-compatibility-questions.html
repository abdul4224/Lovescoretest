50 Fun Compatibility Questions to Ask Your Partner, Crush, or Best Friend

A percentage from a calculator is fun, but real connection is built through actual conversation. Here are 50 genuinely fun questions - grouped by how deep you want to go - for a partner, a crush, or a best friend.

Getting to know each other

  1. What's a small thing that instantly makes your day better?
  2. What's your go-to comfort food?
  3. Are you a morning person or a night owl?
  4. What's a movie or show you could rewatch endlessly?
  5. What's your favorite way to spend a completely free Saturday?
  6. What's a hobby you'd love to pick up if you had more time?
  7. What's your love language, or how do you usually show people you care?
  8. What song instantly puts you in a good mood?
  9. Coffee, tea, or neither?
  10. What's a place you've never been but really want to visit?
  11. What's your idea of a perfect low-key evening?
  12. What's a food you refuse to eat, no matter what?
  13. Do you prefer texting or calling?
  14. What's a skill you're proud of learning?
  15. What's your favorite season and why?

Deeper connection

  1. What does feeling truly supported look like to you?
  2. What's something you've changed your mind about in the last few years?
  3. What's a value you'd never compromise on?
  4. How do you usually handle disagreements?
  5. What's something you're working on improving about yourself?
  6. What does a "good day" look like for you emotionally, not just logistically?
  7. What's a moment you felt truly proud of yourself?
  8. What's something you wish more people understood about you?
  9. How do you like to be comforted when you're stressed?
  10. What does trust mean to you in a relationship or friendship?
  11. What's a goal you're chasing right now?
  12. What's something from your childhood that still shapes how you see things?
  13. What makes you feel most like yourself?
  14. What's a fear you've worked hard to overcome?
  15. What do you need most from the people close to you?

Just for laughs

  1. What's the most embarrassing thing you've ever done in public?
  2. If you could have any superpower for a day, what would it be?
  3. What's a food combination you love that other people think is weird?
  4. What's your most-used emoji?
  5. If you had a theme song, what would it be?
  6. What's the worst haircut you've ever had?
  7. Would you rather fight one horse-sized duck or a hundred duck-sized horses?
  8. What's a conspiracy theory you secretly enjoy, even if you don't believe it?
  9. What's the weirdest thing you've ever Googled?
  10. If you had to eat one meal for the rest of your life, what would it be?
  11. What's your most irrational fear?
  12. What's a trend you never understood?
  13. What's the last thing that made you laugh until it hurt?
  14. If you were a kitchen appliance, which one would you be, and why?
  15. What's your go-to karaoke song, even if you can't sing?

For couples specifically

  1. What made you realize you liked me?
  2. What's a small habit of mine that makes you smile?
  3. What's something you want us to try together this year?
  4. What does feeling loved look like for you on an ordinary Tuesday?
  5. What's a memory of us you think about often?

Pick a handful, or work through the whole list over a few conversations. There's no scoring system here - the point is just to actually talk.
