How this calculator works

This tool takes the date you enter and today's date from your device, then works out the exact calendar difference between them — years, months, days, full weeks, and total days. It isn't a rough estimate based on 365-day years; it accounts for actual month lengths and leap years, so the numbers line up with a real calendar.

1. Enter your start date

The day you got together, met, or whatever date matters to the two of you.

2. See your exact time together

3. Check your next milestone

The tool finds the next round day-count milestone — like 100, 365, or 1000 days — and shows how far away it is.

A quick honest note

A days-together counter is a nice way to notice time passing and maybe plan something for a milestone, but it's not a measure of how healthy or strong a relationship is. Two people together for 100 days and two people together for 10 years can both be doing great, or both be struggling — the number itself doesn't say which.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the total time together calculated?

The calculator takes the date you enter and today's date and works out the exact number of days, weeks, months, and years between them using calendar math, not a rough estimate.

What counts as a milestone?

The tool highlights common day-count milestones people celebrate, such as 100 days, 365 days, 1000 days, and every 500 or 1000 days after that, and shows how many days remain until the next one.

How is the next anniversary worked out?

It finds the next occurrence of your start date's month and day from today. If your anniversary falls on February 29th, it's shown on February 28th in years that aren't leap years.

Is my date stored anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser using today's date from your device's clock. Nothing is sent to a server or saved.

Can I use this for a friendship or any other date, not just a relationship?

Yes. Enter any start date - a friendship, a job, living somewhere - and it will calculate the same way. The labels just default to relationship-style wording.

The Relationship Days Calculator is a free tool. It performs calendar math only and does not assess relationship health.
