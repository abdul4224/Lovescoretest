Understanding Dr. Gary Chapman's 5 Love Styles

Every individual expresses and interprets emotional intimacy through a primary emotional lens. Misunderstandings happen when partners speak different emotional dialects.

1. Words of Affirmation

Verbal compliments, words of appreciation, and encouraging text messages strengthen connection.

2. Quality Time

Undivided attention without smartphone distractions, late-night deep talks, and shared memories.

3. Physical Touch

Holding hands, hugs, gentle shoulder touches, and physical closeness build security.

4. Acts of Service

Actions speak louder than words—cooking a meal, helping with chores, easing partner's daily stress.

5. Receiving Gifts

Thoughtful visual symbols of love and effort that demonstrate you were thinking of them.
