Love Language Test: Discover Your Giving & Receiving Styles

Find out how you most naturally give and receive affection across the 5 recognized love languages.

Based on the five classic love languages (Words of Affirmation, Acts of Service, Receiving Gifts, Quality Time, and Physical Touch), this test pinpoints your unique affection profile with percentage scores.

How it works

  1. Select your heartfelt preferences across 10 distinct relationship situations
  2. The test tallies weighted scores across all 5 affection channels
  3. Get your Primary & Secondary Love Language breakdown with practical partner tips

What your result means

Your primary language is your heart’s main emotional dialect. Understanding it helps you express your needs and appreciate your partner’s gestures.

Frequently asked questions

Can a person have more than one primary love language?

Yes! Many people have two love languages with very close scores (e.g., Quality Time and Physical Touch).
