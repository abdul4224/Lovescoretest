How Well Do You Know Your Partner? Couple Trivia Challenge

How well do you really know your partner’s favorite comforts, hidden pet peeves, dreams, and childhood memories?

An engaging quiz game to test your mutual intimacy and detail retention. Play alone to test yourself, or take turns passing the device on date night!

How it works

  1. Answer 10 fun trivia questions about your partner’s habits and preferences
  2. Calculate your score and review which answers show deep connection
  3. Receive a Partner IQ certificate title and hilarious conversation prompts

What your result means

A fun gauge of emotional attunement. Any question you miss is simply a great excuse to ask your partner tonight!

Frequently asked questions

Can we play this as a 2-player date game?

Absolutely! One person guesses while the other verifies, then swap roles.
